Paper & ink +

Printed with pigment inks on 310gsm archival cotton rag with a soft matte tooth — the same warm white as Laurita's original paper. Originals are drawn in permanent India ink.

Framing +

The framed option comes in a hand-finished oak frame with anti-reflective museum glass and an acid-free mount. Unframed prints arrive flat or rolled, ready for your own framer.

Shipping & returns +

Prints are made to order and dispatched within 5–7 working days. Free shipping within South Africa over R 1 500; worldwide shipping calculated at checkout. Originals are couriered insured.
